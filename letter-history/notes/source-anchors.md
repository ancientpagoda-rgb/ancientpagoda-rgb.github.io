# Source Anchors

- Britannica's alphabet history overview is a useful starting source for the
  Canaanite/Phoenician, Greek, Etruscan, and Latin branches.
- Proto-Sinaitic and Proto-Canaanite histories are debated and fragmentary; use
  confidence labels for pictorial origins.
- Latin letter histories are not all direct one-line descents. C/G, I/J, U/V/W,
  and Y/Z are especially useful examples of splitting, borrowing, dormancy, and
  reintroduction.
