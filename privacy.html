Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026. This policy describes the Ancient Pagoda Labs homepage and linked interactive projects.

What we collect

The homepage does not require an account and does not intentionally collect names, email addresses, precise location, or device fingerprints. Interactive projects may process controls and simulation state in your browser. We do not request device-location access.

Storage and third-party resources

The site may use essential browser storage for privacy preferences. Embedded project pages and their own providers may have separate data practices. The homepage uses resources hosted on this site and may load Google advertising technology after the applicable consent flow.

Advertising

Ancient Pagoda Labs is configured to use Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies, device information, approximate location inferred from network information, and similar information to provide, measure, and protect advertising. Where consent is required, Google’s certified consent-management flow controls the advertising choice before personalized advertising is used.

See How Google uses information from sites and apps and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices

You can reject optional advertising storage where the consent message offers that choice, or reopen the consent controls with the control. You can also clear site storage through your browser settings.

The Google consent controls are supplied by Google when available. Please reload the page and use the consent message’s settings control.

For corrections or privacy questions, use the project’s GitHub repository without posting sensitive personal information.
